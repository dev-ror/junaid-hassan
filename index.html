About

An engineer who's owned Shopify Plus builds end to end

I'm an expert-level Shopify & Shopify Plus consultant with over a decade of full-stack e-commerce development experience. I work across the entire lifecycle: custom theme builds from a blank canvas, Online Store 1.0 → 2.0 migrations, cross-platform moves onto Shopify, and end-to-end store setup and launch.

Beyond the storefront, I build the plumbing merchants don't see — custom and public Shopify apps, Checkout Extensions, and bespoke middleware that connects Shopify to inventory systems, marketing platforms, and support channels. I've led engineering teams delivering scalable, high-performing storefronts for enterprise Shopify Plus merchants across North America and Australasia, with additional depth in React.js, Angular, Vue.js, Nest.js, and Ruby on Rails.

What I Build

Apps & integrations, shipped inside Shopify

Alongside storefronts, I design and ship purpose-built Shopify apps that solve a specific operational problem for a merchant — then wire them into the rest of the stack with custom middleware.

  • Mailbot

    Lifecycle & transactional email

    A custom automation engine that triggers lifecycle and transactional email flows from Shopify events — cart, order, fulfillment, and win-back sequences — without relying on a third-party platform for the logic.

    Scaleadd: stores served / emails sent per month

  • Pushbot

    Web push notifications

    A push-notification engine for cart recovery, back-in-stock alerts, and promotional campaigns, built to fire directly off Shopify store events in real time.

    Scaleadd: subscribers / notifications sent per month

  • WhatsApp Integration

    Conversational commerce

    Order confirmations, shipping updates, cart recovery, and support conversations routed through WhatsApp, connected to Shopify order and customer data via custom middleware.

    Scaleadd: messages sent per month / stores using it

  • Feed App

    Product feed automation

    An app that generates and continuously syncs product feeds from Shopify to advertising and marketplace channels, keeping pricing, inventory, and availability accurate without manual exports.

    Scaleadd: SKUs / channels synced

  • Sort App

    Merchandising & collection rules

    A custom sorting engine that applies merchandising rules across collections at scale — inventory-aware, rule-based, and built to update automatically as stock and demand shift.

    Scaleadd: collections / SKUs managed

  • Centralized SSO

    Identity & access

    A single sign-on layer that unifies login across multiple storefronts and brand properties under one account system, reducing friction for multi-brand and multi-region merchants.

    Scaleadd: storefronts / brands unified
